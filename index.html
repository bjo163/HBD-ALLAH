RESEARCH GOVERNANCE

Methodology & Evidence

Scores compare concepts against an explicitly chosen reference; they do not measure which religion is true or accepted by God.

01 / THE FORMULA

Q4Q 150-point framework

Each dimension is scored from 0 through 10, then multiplied by its fixed weight.

8T + 4A + 2R + MMaximum 80 + 40 + 20 + 10 = 150.

U (Universe) and RU (Ruh) are descriptive domains. They are not additional weighted points.

02 / IDENTITY

Major means registry root

Branches, denominations, schools and practices have their own IDs and parent IDs, but are not added as separate root religions on the major board.

Historical relatives can be separate roots. A historical family like “Abrahamic” is never a parent_id.

03 / PROVENANCE

Observation before interpretation

OBSERVATION → CLASSIFICATION → RELATION → INTERPRETATION → EVIDENCE → VALIDATION.

Similarity is not shared origin. Association is not causation. Missing data is not zero.

04 / STATUS

What makes a score publishable?

Traceable passages, justified subscore, competing interpretations, community context, independent review, and versioned methodology.

Legacy / Draft / Reviewed / Validated are separate states; a local draft never becomes validated automatically.
